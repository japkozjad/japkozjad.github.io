Basically yes! However, you must credit me under all conditions, no matter if it's for TikTok Dance Video or for a Twitter/X, Mastodon or BlueSky funny video. So, if you'll use my music, please credit me by my artistic name (Japkozjad) and add a link to the source if you're going to publish it.


For a YouTube Video, please use this:

Listen on YouTube: (here you put the link)

Important for content creators! I am not using any automatic copyright strike systems. If you recieve a copyright strike by using my music, please contact with my using my social media or via mail.

What about streaming services?

The same as in answer above. Just don't forget to put info in the description/during the stream:

Music provided by Japkozjad

Art Usage:

Can I use your art as my profile picture (PFP) or banner?

Sure! Go ahead! You don't even have to ask. However, providing a small credit in your bio (e.g., "PFP by Japkozjad") is highly appreciated and helps me out a lot! .3.

Can I repost your artwork on other websites?

Yes, but under two conditions: always credit me as the artist (Japkozjad) and provide a direct link back to the original post (e.g., to my website, Bluesky, or Derpibooru). Do not claim my art as your own.

Can I use your art in my YouTube video / presentation / edit?

Basically yes, as long as it's a non-commercial project! Just make sure to put a clear credit in the description or somewhere in the video so people know who drew it.

Before using my art for any commercial purposes or paid advertisements, please contact me first via mail or social media.

Can I make a redraw or trace your art to practice?

For private practice? Absolutely! If you want to publish a redraw/speedpaint, just clearly state that it's based on my original artwork and provide a link to it.

Can I use your artwork for AI training or AI generation?

Absolutely NOT. The exact same rule applies here as for my music. I strictly forbid using any of my artworks, characters, or assets for generative AI models.
