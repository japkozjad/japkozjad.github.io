Whitelush is a laid-back, composed observer and a pacifist with a dry sense of humor. He can’t stand aggression or loud places. He’s a master at reading body language, able to flawlessly sense a lie or sadness from just a single subtle movement of a hoof or wing. He consciously rejects having a cutie mark, valuing his freedom. Normally, he may seem like a distant outsider, but in personal situations, he becomes extremely shy. It’s easy to fluster or embarrass him with displays of affection. Once he forms a deeper relationship with someone, he becomes fiercely loyal and deeply attached.
