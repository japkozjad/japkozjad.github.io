## My vision of Equestria
My vision of Equestria is a bit different than in the series. It includes basic stuff like magic, harmony or friendship, yet it adds modern technology, lifestyle and trends (stuck in 2010s-2020s).

In this world, smartphones, tablets, computers, cars and Internet are used by most creatures every day and often they are the part of their jobs. Most of technology from real world is almost identical or just the same.

#### Interaction with devices:
Ponies interact with touchscreens, keyboards and mouses using their hooves, magic or wing feathers. Griffons and dragons like Spike can use them just like humans.

#### Magic vs. Tech:
While traditional magic still exists, technology exists along with it. Magic isn't the only solution to every problem; science and modern engineering have got just as important as magic.

#### Transport:
Carriages and wheelbarrows have been replaced by various types of cars, taxis, vans, and trucks. Although mail carriers and couriers often prefer to use their legs or wings.

The trains run on magnetic tracks, so there's no need to go to the trouble of installing overhead power lines.

Planes are a popular way to travel to places that are far, even pegasi prefer that instead of risking getting tired or catching a cold.

### Economy
#### Currency:
Equestrian citizens use `bits` as a currency. Both cash and digital currency are in circulation, and in many places you can pay with cash, a card, or your phone.

#### Earnings:
The national minimum wage in Equestria is **1,846** `bits`/month after tax, and the national average is approximately **4,215** `bits`/month after tax.

#### Healthcare:
Equestria operates on a universal healthcare system, meaning that basic medical care, hospital visits and specialist consultations in public institutions are complete free for all citizens. However, a private healthcare sector also exists alongside the public one, allowing creatures to pay out-of-pocket or use private medical insurance for faster access to doctor and premium services.

### Weather and climate
#### Natural cycle:
Unlike canon, for the most part, the weather changes on its own, naturally, just as it does in our world. Pegasi no longer have to “arrange the clouds” every day.

#### Emergency situations / Agriculture:
The Weather Bureau intervenes only in emergency situations (e.g., during a drought) or locally to support agriculture (e.g., rain clouds delivery to farmlands). 

#### The Sun and the Moon cycle:
In this universe, the Sun and the Moon cycle changes naturally on its own. Princesses Celestia and Luna view the rising of the sun and moon symbolically, which means that Celestia takes care of Equestria during the day, and Luna does so at night.

### Lifestyle
#### Diet:
In this world, creatures eat the same food as humans (e.g., meat). That's all about the diet.

#### Internet & Social media:
Just like the technology, the Internet in this world is exactly the same as in the real world. Ponies and other creatures use the exact same websites, apps and social media platforms as humans do.

### Towns and cities
The look and structure of towns in this world differ significantly from the canon. While places like Ponyville still retain some of their local, cozy charm, they have adapted to modern infrastructures and urban standards.

#### Infrastructure & Streets:
While town centres maintain cobblestone roads, the rest of the streets have been largely replaced by asphalt ones, concrete sidewalks and proper road markings. Streetlights are standard, illuminating the towns at night in warm yellow color.

#### Architecture & Businesses:
Traditional cottage-style buildings exist alongside modern architecture. Local spots are now contemporary businesses, including electronic repair shops, 24/7 stores, internet cafes, local restaurants and fast-food spots. 

#### Public Services & Commerce:
Every major town has modernized public institutions, such as police stations, municipal clinics, and fully equipped hospitals. Economic life revolves around shopping centers.

#### Atmosphere:
The aesthetics are more like a real small town from the 2010s, blending the hustle and bustle of modern daily life with distinctive local spots where ponies gather after work to spend time together.

### Apply
Apply is a pegasus stallion in his mid-twenties, my main ponysona. He lives with his mother, [Easedrop](#easedrop) and his sister, [Skye](#skye) who are his absolute top priority in life. Among his friends, he's known as a loyal and devoted companion. He's friends with [Sparrow](#sparrow), [Whitelush](#whitelush), [Blaze](#blaze), [Purpleglow](#purpleglow) and [Dingo](#dingo).

#### Personality:
Apply is an introvert with a calm, composed, and empathetic approach to life. He keeps a clear distance from strangers, is quiet and reserved, but around family and friends, he opens up completely and has a great time. He’s very patient and has high personal culture, though he can be stubborn and tends to take some things too personally. As a careful observer, he notices details that others miss.

#### Occupation & Lifestyle:
Apply is a music producer. It’s both his job and his passion. His work focuses on creating instrumentals and beats for artists. He doesn’t make a lot of money, but he’s able to contribute to the bills. Outside of work, he creates his own tracks, which usually never leave his hard drive. His headphones are often playing EDM, hip-hop/trap, metal, and Eurodance. Besides music, he likes to sketch from time to time. He loves a specific, nostalgic atmosphere: walks in the rain, the warm glow of yellow streetlights, and watching sunrises and sunsets while sitting on rooftops or relaxing on the porch. He likes to cook, though he often relies on frozen meals (mainly casseroles). His diet consists mainly of simple, hearty dishes like pizza, fries, burgers, and spaghetti (and, of course, whatever he cooks with his mom).

Apply has Asperger’s syndrome and aortic valve regurgitation since birth, which causes him to tire much more quickly than his peers. He also suffers from claustrophobia and a fear of extreme heights (how ironic). In his daily life, he avoids alcohol (except for the occasional symbolic drink) and hot days. He has a bad tolerance for temperatures above 25°C. He regularly smokes cigarettes and vapes.

### Easedrop
Easedrop is a mature pegasus mare, mother of [Apply](#apply) and [Skye](#skye) and the emotional anchor and pillar of the entire family. She runs her own little shop in town, ensuring the stability and well-being of her children, who mean the world to her. She's the only character described here who has a cutie mark. Her cutie mark represents a falling blue feather, which symbolizes stability and calm despite difficulties.

#### Personality:
Easedrop is intelligent, empathetic, loyal, and has endless patience (until someone really pushes her over the edge). She has a great sense of humor, which allows her to easily connect with the younger generation. Although she's incredibly supportive and warm, she's by no means submissive and takes a strict stance on obedience. She has a strong character; when she sees her loved ones heading down a path of self-destruction or making fatal decisions, she can erupt with powerful, fully justified anger.

#### Occupation & Lifestyle:
Because she runs her own business, Easedrop often takes on too many responsibilities and regularly works overtime. She spends every spare moment making her children happy. Her passion is cooking, especially baking cakes and casseroles and preparing homemade meals. In her free time, she relaxes by sketching and resting in absolute peace and quiet. She hates cheesy romantic comedies.

Her biggest fear in life is that something bad might happen to her children. She has been struggling with nicotine addiction since she was 17. She reaches for cigarettes especially during times of intense stress and overwork.

### Skye
Skye is a young pegasus mare (a teenager or young adult, depending on the scenario). She's the younger sister of [Apply](#apply) and the daughter of [Easedrop](#easedrop). As a teenager, she attends a general high school. As an adult, she works as a barista at the Warm Hoof cafe.

Skye is the complete opposite of her brother. She's a volcano of energy, an incredibly spontaneous, loud, and spirited person. She has a natural curiosity about the world and a tendency to constantly test boundaries and challenge her surroundings. She 's afraid of [FOMO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fear_of_missing_out) and a fear of failure. Musically, she’s most drawn to pop-punk, rock, and metal. Singing is her passion.

When it comes to food, Skye has an absolute hatred of vegetables, however she has a massive sweet tooth for snacks, ice cream, iced coffee, and energy drinks. She almost always wears her black collar around her neck. She doesn’t have any regular vices, though she sometimes sneaks a cigarette from Apple or her mom to smoke (as an adult pony, she doesn’t even try to hide it anymore).

## How Apply met his friends
### Sparrow (school years)
Apply and Sparrow met in 1st grade of high school. As young ponies without cutie marks, they stuck together in their school desks. Sparrow, who was often secretive and unsure of herself in social situations due to her difficult home life, found acceptance, a safe haven, and a common language with Apply, who was a calm and understanding classmate. Sparrow often confessed to him how her grandparents treated her, and Apply tried to cheer her up (clumsily, but it helped).

### Blaze (via Sparrow)
Apply met Blaze through Sparrow during his third year of high school. It all started when Sparrow introduced them, they both started talking about their interests, and things just took off from there. Blaze often saved their butts when other students made fun of their weirdness or generally picked on them. One day, Blaze dragged them along on an urbexing trip to an abandoned train station. When they got caught, Apply stood up for him by lying that they were gathering information for a school project. Blaze was really impressed by that.

### Whitelush (bad day)
They met when Apply was sitting on a park bench. He was having a rough day because he had just returned from his annual visit to the cardiologist and had been told that, due to his heart condition, he couldn't do any heavy physical work. Whitelush, walking through the park, sensed that Apply was quite nervous and downcast. He approached him without judgment and offered his silent presence. After a while, Apply gently opened up to him and Whitelush let him vent. He offered him a CBD joint to help him get through this crisis, not expecting that he had just gained a friend for life.

### Dingo (lost in the forest)
This friendship began with an unfortunate accident, when Apply completely lost his sense of direction and got lost deep in the forest while out for a walk. As if that weren’t enough, his wing was bandaged from the accident and his phone was dead. Due to a heart condition, the pegasus was barely able to catch his breath from exhaustion and stress. Dingo came across the exhausted Apply while out for a walk. At first Apply thought that Dingo will harm him, but Dingo quickly put his doubts aside when he saw the pegasus’s condition. Dingo offered to help him get out of the forest, offered him some tea from a thermos, and made sure Apply got home safely. A few days later, Apply ran into him again at the supermarket while he was looking for the right floats for his fishing rod. They went to the lake to fish, started talking to each other, and that’s how their acquaintance and later, their friendship developed.

### Purpleglow (help in crisis)
While Purpleglow is now a financially independent translator, their first meeting took place when she was at her lowest point and on the brink of absolute poverty. Apply (though he himself had never had much money) selflessly offered her a helping hoof at that critical moment, saving her from debt and a descent into alcoholism. At first, Easedrop wasn’t happy that Apply was spending his last bit of money on her, especially since Purpleglow could be a pain, but over time she began to see Apply’s actions as noble and started treating Purpleglow as a friend. Because Apply didn’t let her fall apart, she considers him one of the closest and most respected friends she ever had in her entire life.

## The Blank Flank Theory
You may wonder why almost all my characters don't have a cutie mark.

#### Real answer:
I had zero ideas what cutie marks would fit them. Blank flanks look good to me anyway.

#### In-world answer:
Some of them ([Apply](#apply), [Skye](#skye), [Purpleglow](#purpleglow), and [Dingo](#dingo)) never got their own cutie mark and with time simply stopped worrying about it, focusing instead on their lives. As for [Blaze](#blaze), [Sparrow](#sparrow) and [Whitelush](#whitelush), they consciously chose not to pursue their cutie marks, not wanting a little symbol to define their destined purpose.
